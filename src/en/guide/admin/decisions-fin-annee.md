# Year-end decisions

At the end of the year, each student moves up, repeats the year or leaves
the school. ERA proposes a decision for each student from their results;
**the administrator alone** accepts or changes it. These decisions are then
used to re-enroll students into the next year.

## Before you start

- **Levels**: each class needs a level, and each level its next level (see
  [Levels](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement#levels)). Tick **Exam
  year** on levels ending in a national exam, and **Last level of the
  school** on the highest one.
- **Each department's pass mark**: in **School setup → Departments**, field
  **Pass mark** (10 by default).
- **The last period closed**: decisions open once the year's last period is
  closed (see
  [Closing a year or a term](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement#closing-a-year-or-a-term)),
  when averages can no longer change. No need to wait for the end of the
  year: June class councils can meet as soon as the last term is closed.
  Before that, the page already shows the proposals, read-only.

## Deciding for a class

1. Open **Students & admissions → Year-end decisions**.
2. Choose the **Academic year**, then the **Class**. The class list shows
   progress, e.g. "12/35 decided".
3. For each student, the page shows their **Annual average** (in orange
   below the pass mark), ERA's **Proposal** with its reason, and the
   recorded **Decision**.

Three ways to record decisions:

- **Accept proposals (n)**: records at once the proposal of every student in
  the class who has no decision yet. Students already decided are never
  changed; those without a proposal remain to decide one by one.
- **Accept proposal**, on a student's row.
- **Decide** (or **Edit**): choose the decision yourself and optionally add
  a **Note**, e.g. "Moved up by the class council".

**Cancel decision** puts the student back to "to decide".

Each decision keeps the annual average it was taken on, its author and its
date.

## How ERA proposes

In this order:

| Situation                                                       | Proposal                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Permanently excluded by a discipline council this year          | Leaves the school                  |
| Class of a level marked **Exam year**                           | None: decide from the exam results |
| Department assessed without numeric grades (nursery, preschool) | Moves up                           |
| No grades this year                                             | None: to decide                    |
| Annual average at or above the pass mark                        | Moves up                           |
| Annual average below the pass mark                              | Repeats the year                   |

An average exactly equal to the pass mark is a pass.

On the school's last level, "Moves up" reads **Passed · leaves the school**:
the student succeeded and leaves.

::: tip A proposal is only a starting point
A student at 9.50 can be moved up by the class council, a student who
passed may go to another school: just change their decision.
:::
