# The parent portal

As a parent or guardian, you have a dedicated space to follow your
children's schooling: grades, attendance, documents to download,
payments and requests. Once signed in, you land on the **My children**
page, which shows one card per child linked to your account, with their
class, average and payment balance. Click a card to open that child's
full record.

This guide covers the three things you can do from this portal:

| Page                                                                       | What you can do there                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Your children and their documents](/en/guide/parent/enfants-et-documents) | See your children, follow their record (grades, attendance, discipline) and download their report cards, receipts, disciplinary-council minutes and lesson-log attachments; export their timetable |
| [Online payments](/en/guide/parent/paiements-en-ligne)                     | Track school fees and pay directly online by mobile money                                                                                                                                          |
| [Requests](/en/guide/parent/demarches)                                     | Justify a child's absence or request a site transfer                                                                                                                                               |

Account settings (password, notifications, language) work the same way
for every role: see [Getting started](/en/guide/prise-en-main).
