# Grades and evaluations

This page covers creating an evaluation and entering the grades attached
to it. If your school grades some classes by competency rather than by
numeric score (typically nursery/pre-school), the last section explains
how to record your observations there.

## Create an evaluation

1. Open **Teaching → Evaluations** in the menu.
2. Click **New evaluation**.
3. In the **Context** section, choose the **Class**, **Subject**,
   **Academic period** and **Evaluation type** — all four lists only offer
   your own class/subject assignments.
4. In the **Details** section, fill in the evaluation's **Label**, its
   **Max score** (20 by default, editable) and its **Date**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Find an evaluation

On the **Evaluations** page, use the search field (by label) and the class
filter to find an evaluation in the list, which shows each one's class,
subject, period and date. Clicking a row opens a detail panel.

## Enter grades

1. From the evaluations list, click **Grades** on the row for the relevant
   evaluation.
2. For each student, enter their **Score** (between 0 and the evaluation's
   max score) and, if applicable, a **Bonus/malus**.
3. Set the student's **Status**: **Graded**, **Absent** or **Excused**.
   The read-only **Current subject average** column shows each student's
   average in the subject before you save.
4. Click **Save** — every row is saved at once.

## Edit or delete an evaluation

From the list, use **Edit** to change an evaluation's context or details.
**Delete** permanently removes it along with **every grade recorded for
it** — a confirmation dialog asks you to confirm before this irreversible
action runs.

## Competency observations

Some departments — typically nursery and pre-school — are graded by
competency rather than by numeric score: the teacher is assigned to the
whole class there, with no separate subject, and the administrator defines
a list of competencies to observe (admin menu **School setup → Competencies**).
If one of your classes belongs to such a department, a **Competency
observations** page is available for it.

1. Open the **Competency observations** page for the relevant class.
2. Choose the **academic period** from the selector at the top of the page
   — the list of students and competencies reloads automatically.
3. For each student and each competency, choose a level: **Not acquired**,
   **In progress** or **Acquired**, and optionally add a comment.
4. Click **Save**.

If the class's department has no competencies defined yet, the page shows
"This department has no competencies to observe" — an administrator needs
to create some before you can record anything.
