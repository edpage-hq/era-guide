# Parents

The **Parents** page, in the **Students & admissions** space, gathers the
students' parents: their contact details, their children and where their
account stands. It is mostly used to fill in missing e-mail addresses and
phone numbers, then to invite the parents into the application.

It is open to the administration and the secretariat. The administration
sees every parent. The secretariat sees the parents of the students
enrolled on **its site**, the one on its employee profile. A secretariat
member with no site on their profile sees every parent.

::: info
Children are not linked to a parent from this page: that is still done
from the student's record, under **Guardians**, by the administration.
:::

## The list

Each row shows the parent, their e-mail address, their children with
their class for the year (series and site included), their phone and
where their account stands:

- **No e-mail**: the parent has no e-mail address yet. This is the case
  for parents brought over from the previous platform without one. They
  cannot be invited until it is entered.
- **Not invited**: the address is known, no invitation has been sent.
- **Invited**: an invitation was sent and its link still works.
- **Invitation expired**: the link expired before the parent used it.
- **Activated**: the parent has chosen their password.

To find a parent, type their name, address, phone or the name of one of
their children, then click **Search**. The **All statuses** and
**All sites** lists (when you see several sites) filter the list.

**Export as CSV** downloads the list as filtered. With the **No e-mail**
filter, it is the list of families to call for their address.

## Correcting the contact details

Click **Edit** at the end of the row.

1. Correct the **Name**, **E-mail address** or **Phone**. The phone is
   written with the country code, for example +228 90 11 22 33.
2. For a parent without an e-mail, leave the field empty until you have
   their address: they keep their **No e-mail** status.
3. Fill in, when known, the **Civility** (Mr., Mrs., Miss), **Profession**,
   **Nationality** and **P.O. box**. The civility precedes the parent's name
   on the Traditional report card; all four fields are also in the CSV
   export.
4. Click **Save changes**.

As soon as a real address is saved, the parent becomes **Not invited** and
can be invited. Changing the address of an account not yet activated
cancels the invitation already sent, since it went to the old address.

The **Children** section lists their children and their class. The
administration finds a link there to the **Student's guardians**.

## Inviting the parent

In the **Access to the application** section:

- **Send the invitation** e-mails the parent a link to choose their
  password. The link stays valid for 7 days. For a parent already invited,
  the button becomes **Resend the invitation**, and the new link replaces
  the old one.
- **Copy an invitation link**, if the e-mail does not arrive: the link is
  shown and copied, to hand to the parent another way. It replaces the one
  sent by e-mail.

A parent who has activated their account no longer needs an invitation:
if they forget their password, they use **Forgot password** on the sign-in
page.
