# Digitisation

ERA brings paper in two ways: **photographing the pages with a phone**,
for a document handed to you at the desk, and **archive recovery**, for a
whole box of old files.

Either way, ERA **reads the text** on the images, so the document can be
found by its content later. The reading happens in your browser: the
document never leaves the school.

::: info First reading
The first time a browser reads a text, it downloads the reading engine
and the French and English models (a few MB), then keeps them.
That first reading needs an internet connection and takes a little
longer.
:::

Reading makes documents **searchable** and **suggests the people** whose
name appears. It does not fill in fields for you: always check what you
classify.

## Photograph a document with a phone

1. In the file, click **File** next to the type concerned.
2. Choose **Photos of the pages**.
3. Click **Take a photo** and photograph the first page, flat and well
   lit. Repeat for each page, in order. **Choose images** takes photos
   already on the phone.
4. Each page shows as a numbered thumbnail. The cross removes a bad page.
   The text of each page is read while you carry on.
5. Fill in the title, date and notes if needed, then click **File**.

The pages, 20 at most, are gathered into **one PDF** in A4 format, in the
order you took them. The document then goes the way of any filing:
validated at once if you may validate it and the box is ticked, otherwise
waiting to be checked.

In **A file** mode, the text of an image filed is read the same way. The
text of PDFs is not read.

## Find a document by its content

On the **Documents** page, the **Search the documents** field also
searches the text read. Type for example a place of birth or a
certificate number to find the documents that carry it.

## Archive recovery

A **recovery campaign** brings a paper archive in bulk, then classifies
it document by document. It is for an administrator and the document
manager, from the **Documents** space → **Archive recovery**.

### Create a campaign

Under **New campaign**, name it after the archive it brings in (for
example "Enrolment files 2019–2020"), add notes if needed, then click
**Create the campaign**.

The **Campaigns** list shows, for each, how many scans it holds and how
many are left to classify.

### Bring the scans in

Scan the box with the school's scanner or a phone, then, in the campaign,
click **Choose the scans** and select all the files at once: PDF or
images, 10 MB each at most.

ERA reads the text on each image then sends it, one at a time. The
counter shows how far it has got. Keep the page open until the end. If a
scan could not be sent, the counter says so: choose it again.

The scans brought in wait under **To classify**. Until they are
classified they have no type and no file: only an administrator and the
document manager see them, and they show neither in the files nor among
the documents to check.

### Classify a scan

The campaign shows the first scan to classify, with the text read on it
under **Text read on the page**. The numbers above it let you pick
another.

1. Choose the **Document type**.
2. If the type goes in a student's or an employee's file, ERA suggests
   the people whose name is on the page, and ticks the first. Check it,
   or search for someone else by name with the magnifying glass.
3. Fill in the **Title** and the **Date of the document** if needed.
4. If you may validate this type, the **I have checked it: validate it
   now** box is ticked. Otherwise the document waits to be checked by
   those who validate this type.
5. Click **Classify**. The next scan shows.

For a student, ERA suggests those whose **last name and first given
name** are on the page, or whose registration number is, when the school
uses them. For an employee, their full name is needed. Accents and
capitals do not matter. A last name alone is not enough: it too often
belongs to several students.

The last scans classified show at the bottom of the campaign, with their
type, person and status. Once classified, a scan is a document like any
other, in its file.
