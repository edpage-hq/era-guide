# Enrollment and payments

This page covers the two moments where the cashier's office steps into
a student's journey: entering a new application for a family visiting
in person, and following the financial side of an already-enrolled
student — fees, payments, discounts, and payment plan — under
**Billing**.

## Entering an application for a family on site

When a family visits in person instead of filling in the online form,
you're the one who submits their application.

1. Open **New application** in the left-hand menu.
2. Enter the student's **First name**, **Last name**, and **Date of
   birth**, then the desired **Site** and **Department**.
3. Add at least one **Parent / guardian**: first name, last name,
   email, phone (optional), and relationship (**Father**, **Mother**,
   **Legal guardian**, or **Other**). Check **Primary contact** for the
   person to contact first. Use **Add guardian** to enter more than
   one.
4. Click **Submit application**.

This application joins the same queue as applications received online,
tagged with the **Cashier** channel — the registrar's office reviews it
and decides whether to assign it to a class. See
[Applications](/en/guide/secretariat/candidatures).

::: tip Offline mode
If your workstation has no connection at the time, the form still
works: the application is queued and sent automatically once the
connection returns. You cannot attach documents while offline, though —
add them once you're back online. A banner at the top of the page shows
how many applications are waiting to sync, with a **Sync now** button
as soon as you're back online.
:::

## Tracking a student's fees and payments

Once an application is approved by the registrar's office, the student
appears under **Billing**. The list can be filtered by student name and
academic year, and shows each student's total due, total paid, and
remaining balance. Click **View** to open a student's full financial
record.

The detail page shows four key figures: **Total due**, **Discount**,
**Total paid**, and **Balance**.

### Setting the fees due

If a fee structure exists for the student's site, department, and
academic year, it appears under **Fee items**: mandatory items are
listed directly, and optional items (for example cafeteria or
transport) are offered as checkboxes — check the ones that apply to
this student, then click **Save changes**.

If no fee structure is configured for this site, department, and
academic year, the page tells you so — in that case, ask an
administrator to create one before you can bill the student.

### Collecting a payment

1. In the **Record a payment** form, enter the **Amount**.
2. Choose the **Method**: Mixx by Yass, Flooz, T-Money, Card, or Bank
   deposit.
3. If a fee structure exists, indicate which **Fee item** this payment
   relates to, or leave the **Lump sum (not tied to an item)** choice
   for a payment that doesn't match any specific item.
4. Fill in the **Reference** (transaction number, check number, etc.)
   if you have one, the date the payment was **paid**, and any
   **Notes**.
5. Click **Record payment**.

The payment appears in the student's **Payments** list, with a receipt
generated automatically — click **Download receipt** on the payment's
row to print it or hand it to the family. The student's guardians also
receive a notification about the payment and the remaining balance.

To remove a payment entered by mistake, click **Delete** on its row and
confirm — this action is permanent.

::: tip Offline mode
A payment can be collected even without a connection: it appears in a
**Pending sync** section until the connection returns, then syncs
automatically (or via **Sync now**).
:::

### Setting up a payment plan

To spread a student's fees over several installments instead of
waiting for a single payment:

1. In the **Payment plan** section, click **Add installment** for each
   installment you want.
2. For each installment, fill in a **Label** (for example "1st
   installment"), an **Amount**, and a **Due date**.
3. Click **Save payment plan**.

Each installment then shows a status: **Upcoming**, **Paid**, or
**Overdue**, based on the due date and payments already received.
Setting up a new plan automatically replaces the previous one, if there
was one. To delete a plan without replacing it, click **Delete** above
the installments table and confirm.

### Viewing and applying a discount

Discounts granted to a student (label, fixed amount or percentage,
reason, and who granted it) are visible to everyone in the
**Discounts** section of the financial record.

::: warning
Granting or removing a discount is reserved for administrator accounts.
If you're signed in as cashier, you'll see existing discounts but not
the **Grant a discount** form — ask an administrator to open the same
record to add one:

1. Enter a **Label** for the discount (what it represents).
2. Choose the **Type**: **Fixed amount** or **Percentage**.
3. Enter the corresponding **Value**.
4. Enter the **Reason** for the discount (optional).
5. Click **Grant discount**.
   :::

A granted discount immediately reduces the **Total due** shown on the
page.
