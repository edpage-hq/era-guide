# Attendance and absences

This page covers taking daily attendance for classes and processing
absence or lateness justifications submitted by families from their
parent portal.

## Take daily attendance

1. Open **School life → Attendance**. The list shows all your classes,
   with a **Today's roll call** badge showing how many students have
   already been marked out of the total enrolled (for example "18/24
   taken").
2. Click **Take attendance** on the row for the relevant class.
3. The page defaults to the current day; change the date at the top of the
   page if you need to complete or correct attendance for another day.
4. For each student, set the **Status**: **Present**, **Absent** or
   **Late**.
   - If you choose **Late**, enter the number of **Minutes late**.
   - Check **Justified** if the absence or lateness is already covered by
     a known reason, and add a free-text **Note** if needed (for example
     "medical certificate provided").
5. Click **Save** — the whole class's attendance is saved at once.

## Process absence justifications

Families submit their absence or lateness justifications from their
parent portal; these appear in **School life → Absence justifications**
with the status **Pending**.

1. Open **Absence justifications**. The list shows, for each request, the
   student, their class, the date concerned, the reason given and its
   status. Click a row to open its detail.
2. If a document was attached, download it via **Download document** to
   review it before making a decision.
3. On a **pending** request, choose:
   - **Approve** — the justification is validated immediately, with no
     further input needed;
   - **Reject** — a dialog asks you to enter a **Reason for rejection**,
     required, before confirming.

Once processed, the request moves to **Approved** or **Rejected**; a
rejected justification shows its rejection reason directly in the list.
