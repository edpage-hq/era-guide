# Students and guardians

This section covers a student's record and everything attached to it:
their class enrollment, their guardians (parents), their possible personal
access to the student portal, and their specialty subjects.

## Student records

**Students & admissions → Students** lists the school's students, with a
search by name (and by registration number, if
[turned on](parametres-application.md#student-records)) and a filter by class (including "Unassigned" for students
with no class in the current year).

Click a student's row to open their **preview**, to the right of the list
on a large screen, or at the bottom of the screen on a phone. Without
leaving the list, it gathers: the class and site, the linked parents or
guardians with their email address, the year's fees (total paid and
balance), attendance (absences, unjustified absences, lates) and the last
three grades. Three buttons lead straight on: **Edit**,
**Parents / guardians** and **Billing**.

To create a student:

1. Click **New student**.
2. Fill in the **First name** and **Last name**, then, when known, the
   **Date of birth** and **Place of birth**. If the school uses
   registration numbers, also fill in the **Registration number**: no two
   students can share one. All these fields are optional; the registration
   number and place of birth are printed on transcripts and certificates
   of completion.
3. Choose a **Class** for the current academic year, or leave it
   unassigned to create the record without an immediate enrollment. The
   list shows each class's head count and capacity.
4. Click **Save**.

A **full** class is not refused here. A notice appears with a **Reason for
going over capacity (optional)** field: the enrollment goes through, the
overrun is recorded (it shows among the
[classes over capacity in pilotage](/en/guide/direction/pilotage)) and the
site's direction is told.

Editing a student shows the same fields, with one difference for the
class. A student with **no class** this academic year can be enrolled
directly. An **already enrolled** student shows their class with a
**Change class** button: moving them goes through a
[class change](/en/guide/secretariat/changements-de-classe), which checks
the site and capacity and records the reason. The form never takes a
student out of their class, since their enrollment carries their payments
and bulletins.

The record also shows two read-only tables if the student has history:

- **Enrollment history** — the classes attended in previous academic
  years.
- **Curriculum history** — every move of the student from one class to
  another during a year (class change, site transfer), with the date, who
  made it, and each class's department and site — so a change of
  pedagogical curriculum (French ↔ Togolese) shows there too.

## A student leaving during the year

A student is **never deleted**: their record carries their grades,
bulletins, payments and history. When a student leaves the school during
the year (moving house, changing school…), record their departure from
their record, **Leaving the school** section:

1. Enter the **Date of departure** (today by default).
2. Write the **Reason**.
3. Click **Record the departure**.

From then on:

- the student drops out of class lists, roll calls, grade sheets,
  bulletin publishing, year-end decisions and re-enrollment;
- they no longer count in their class's head count: their seat is free;
- their boarding bed and bus seat are freed;
- they stay in the student list, greyed out, marked "Left on…";
- the cash desk still sees them, with the same mark: what they owe stays
  owed.

If the student was already enrolled for next year, that enrollment is
marked as left too. A departure recorded by mistake is undone with
**Cancel the departure**: the student is back in their class. Their bed and
bus seat are not given back automatically: they may have gone to someone
else in the meantime.

A student who comes back later is enrolled again, through an admission or
from their record: their record is the same.

## Screens attached to the record

From a student's record, three buttons open dedicated screens: **Specialty
subjects**, **Guardians**, and **Student account** (detailed below).

## Guardians (parents)

From a student's record, click **Guardians** to manage who has parent
portal access for this student.

To add a guardian:

1. Click **Add guardian**.
2. Fill in the **Name**, **Email**, **Relationship** (Father, Mother,
   Legal guardian, or Other), and check **Primary contact** if they're the
   main point of contact.
3. Repeat for each additional guardian (**Remove** button to drop one from
   the form).
4. Click **Save changes**.

Each guardian attached this way gets parent portal access to this student
and to any other student they're linked to elsewhere.

## Student account (student portal)

From a student's record, click **Student account**. Personal access to
the student portal is only possible for a student enrolled in high school
or above — for others, the page shows an ineligibility message and
nothing else.

For an eligible student:

- **Create account** — enter an email address and click **Create
  account**. The student gets a welcome email with a link to choose their
  password (valid for 60 minutes; past that, "Forgot your password?" on
  the sign-in page).
- **Update email** — if an account already exists, change the address and
  click **Update email**.
- **Revoke access** — removes the student's personal access without
  touching their record.

## Specialty subjects

From a student's record, click **Specialty subjects**. This screen only
shows something if the student has an active enrollment and their
department has subjects marked "specialty" (see
[School structure](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement)).

For each specialty subject offered:

1. Check the box to select it for this student.
2. Optionally fill in a personal **coefficient** that replaces the
   department's default — otherwise the department's default coefficient
   applies.
3. Click **Save changes**.
