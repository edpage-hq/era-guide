# Homepage

The homepage is what families and visitors see when they reach your
school's address, before signing in. It is configured from **Administration → Homepage**.

It can contain four parts:

| Part                           | Content                                                     | When it is shown                              |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Header**                     | Logo, tagline, subtitle and buttons                         | Always                                        |
| **About**                      | A title, some text and an image                             | Once it has content, if **Show** is ticked    |
| **Departments**                | Cards (title, description, image) presenting what you offer | Once it has a card, if **Show** is ticked     |
| **Frequently asked questions** | Questions and their answers, which open on click            | Once it has a question, if **Show** is ticked |

The **View page** button, top right, opens the homepage in a new tab so
you can check the result.

## What visitors see while nothing is configured

An empty section never appears: no sample text is ever shown to families.
Without configuration, the page is therefore just the header, with your
logo, the tagline and two buttons.

As long as no section is shown, a banner appears at the top of the
homepage for **administrators only**: "Your homepage only shows the
essentials". Its **Set it up** button leads to the editor. Visitors don't
see it.

## Tracking each part's state

At the top of the editor, four tiles sum up each part's state; the same
state is repeated at the top of each block:

- **Always shown**: the header;
- **Shown**: the part has content and appears on the page;
- **Hidden**: the part has content, but **Show this section on the page**
  is unticked;
- **Empty, not shown**: there is nothing to show yet.

Hiding a part lets you prepare it at your own pace, for instance writing
the whole FAQ, before publishing it by ticking the box.

## Writing in several languages

Each text is entered once per app language (fields carry an **FR** or
**EN** label). You can fill in just one: a visitor browsing the page in
the other language then sees that text rather than an empty part.

## Header

- **Logo**: this is the school's logo, the same one as in the menu. It is
  changed in the settings (**Change the logo** button), see
  [App settings](/en/guide/admin/parametres-application).
- **Tagline** and **Subtitle**: the two lines shown below the logo. Leave
  a field empty to keep the default text in that language.
- **Buttons**: they aren't configurable.
  - **Enrol my child** opens the online enrolment form. Requests sent
    reach the office, see [Applications](/en/guide/secretariat/candidatures).
  - **Log in** leads to the sign-in page. A user who is already signed in
    sees **My space** instead.

Click **Save changes** to apply the tagline and subtitle.

## About

The part where you introduce the school: its history, its vision, what
sets it apart.

1. Tick **Show this section on the page**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Text**. Leave a blank line between two
   paragraphs.
3. Optionally add an **Image** with **Choose an image**: PNG, JPG or WebP,
   4 MB maximum. Landscape works best.
4. Click **Save changes**.

The image is only uploaded when you save. **Replace** picks another one;
**Remove image** deletes it, also on the next save. Without an image, the
text is centred across the full width.

## Departments

One card per department, track or cycle. Cards are **free-form**: they
aren't linked to the departments in
[School structure](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement). You can
therefore present your offer the way you want, for instance a single
"Preschool and primary" card, or a card for an option that isn't a
department.

### Adding a card

1. Click **Add a card**.
2. Fill in the **Title** (required, in at least one language), the
   **Description** and, if you wish, an **Image**.
3. Click **Save**.

A card without an image shows the first letter of its title on a
background in the school's colors.

### Organising cards

- The **Move up** and **Move down** arrows change the display order.
- **Edit** reopens the card; **Delete** removes it permanently, along with
  its image.
- **Section title** replaces "Our departments"; leave it empty to keep
  that title.

## Frequently asked questions

The questions families ask most often: school hours, canteen, payment
arrangements, enrolment steps…

1. Click **Add a question**.
2. Fill in the **Question** and the **Answer**, both required in at least
   one language.
3. Click **Save**.

On the homepage, only the questions are visible; the answer opens when
the visitor clicks on it. The arrows, **Edit**, **Delete** and the
**Section title** work as they do for departments.

::: tip
Saving is immediate: as soon as you click **Save**, the public homepage is
up to date.
:::
