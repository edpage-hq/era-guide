# The parent portal

As a parent or guardian, you have a dedicated space to follow your
children's schooling: grades, attendance, documents to download,
payments and requests. Once signed in, you land on the **My children**
page, which shows one card per child linked to your account: their class,
absences to justify, next installment, average, balance and latest grades. Click a card to open that child's
full record.

This guide covers the three things you can do from this portal:

| Page                                                                       | What you can do there                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Your children and their documents](/en/guide/parent/enfants-et-documents) | See your children, follow their record (grades, attendance, discipline) and download their report cards, receipts, disciplinary-council minutes and lesson-log attachments; export their timetable |
| [Online payments](/en/guide/parent/paiements-en-ligne)                     | Track school fees and pay directly online by mobile money                                                                                                                                          |
| [Requests](/en/guide/parent/demarches)                                     | Justify a child's absence or request a site transfer                                                                                                                                               |

## What you are told about

The school tells you, by email and in the notification bell of your space,
in these cases:

| You are told when…                                       | What it gives you                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| a period's report card is published                      | the link to your child's page              |
| your child is marked absent without justification        | the date and class, so you can justify it  |
| your absence justification is accepted or refused        | the decision, and the reason for a refusal |
| a discipline incident is recorded                        | the date and severity                      |
| a disciplinary council is convened, then once it decides | the session date, then the decision        |
| your child is sent home by the infirmary                 | the reason and time of the visit           |
| a payment is recorded                                    | the amount and the remaining balance       |

You are not told of every grade: the period's grades come with the report
card, and averages can be checked any time on your child's page.

Account settings (password, notifications, language) work the same way
for every role: see [Getting started](/en/guide/prise-en-main).
