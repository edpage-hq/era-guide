# Requests

This page covers two requests you can make yourself for a child, without
going through the school office: justifying an absence and requesting a
site transfer.

## Justifying an absence

When your child has been marked absent or late, you can send a
justification directly from their record:

1. On the child's record, go to the **Attendance** section.
2. Find the row for the day in question (status **Absent** or **Late**)
   and click **Justify**.
3. In the **Justify this absence** window, enter the **Reason**.
4. If you have a supporting document (a medical certificate, for
   example), attach it under **Supporting document** — accepted formats:
   PDF, JPG or PNG. This field is optional.
5. Click **Send**.

Your justification then shows the status **Awaiting review**. The school
reviews it and moves it to **Justified** or **Rejected**; the status
updates directly on your child's record.

## Requesting a site transfer

If you want to enroll your child at another site of the school (a
different campus, for example), you can submit a transfer request:

1. On the child's record, go to the **Inter-site mobility** section.
2. Click **Request a transfer** — this button is only available if no
   request is already pending for this child.
3. In the **Request an inter-site transfer** window, choose the
   **Destination site** and **Destination department** you want.
4. Add a **Reason** if you wish (optional).
5. Click **Send request**.

The request appears in the table below with the date, origin site,
destination site and its status: **Pending**, **Approved** or
**Rejected**. While a request is pending, you cannot submit a new one for
the same child.
