# Course element marks (higher education)

In a higher education programme, you mark **course elements**, promotion
by promotion. The programme head assigns them to you from each promotion's
**Teaching** page.

Your elements are under **Higher education → Course element marks**. Each
line gives the promotion, the unit and the period, with two sheets: **First
session** and **Retake**.

## The first session

The sheet lists the students who take the element: the whole promotion for
a mandatory unit, only those registered for an elective one.

For each, enter out of 20:

- the **continuous assessment**;
- the **exam**, or tick **Absent**: an absence at the exam counts as 0.

The **Element mark** column is worked out as you type, from the share of
continuous assessment the programme sets (40% by default). It shows in
orange below the pass mark. Click **Save marks**.

An emptied line erases the student's marks: it is not a zero.

::: tip Depending on the programme
Some programmes mark their elements on continuous assessment alone or on
the exam alone: the sheet then shows only the column that counts.
:::

## The retake

The retake sheet only lists the students who sit it: their unit is not
validated after the first session (compensation included) and their mark
in this element is below the pass mark.

Enter the **retake exam** mark, or tick **Absent**. It replaces the first
session's exam; the continuous assessment stands. The programme then
decides which mark the element keeps: the retake's, the better of the two,
or the retake's capped.

## Students in debt

A sheet may also list students of a higher promotion who still owe the
unit: they are flagged **Debt** with their promotion's name. Mark them
like the others, at the first session and at the retake. Their mark is
frozen once the jury of their own promotion has deliberated.

## A closed year

Once the year is closed, its sheets can still be read but no longer
changed.
