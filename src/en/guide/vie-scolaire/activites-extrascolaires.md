# Extracurricular activities

This page covers taking attendance for extracurricular activity sessions
(swimming, drama, etc.). The activities and their sessions are created by
the administrator — see
[Extracurricular activities (admin)](/en/guide/admin/activites-extrascolaires)
— this page only covers taking attendance.

## Take attendance for a session

1. Open **School life → Extracurricular attendance**. The list shows the
   scheduled sessions, with the activity, site, department and the
   session's day/time.
2. Click **Take attendance** on the relevant session.
3. Change the date at the top of the page if you need to take attendance
   for an occurrence of the session other than the current day's.
4. For each student enrolled in this session's level at this site, tap
   **Present** or **Absent**.
5. Click **Save**.

If no session is scheduled, or no student is enrolled in the relevant
level at this site, the page says so.

::: tip A roll call built for phones
Each student has a **Present** / **Absent** switch; everyone starts as
**Present**, and the **Everyone present** button resets the whole list to that
status. The counters above the list update on every tap, and the bottom
bar shows "Unsaved changes" until you click **Save** — leaving the page
or changing the date at that point asks for confirmation. Attendance
can't be recorded for a future date.
:::
