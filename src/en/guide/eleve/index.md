# Your student space

As a student, you have your own dashboard in ERA to follow your grades,
classes and documents. This guide covers everything you can view and
download there.

## Your dashboard

Once signed in, you land on the **My portal** page, showing your name,
class and site. If you have no current enrollment, a message tells you
so and the rest of the sections don't display.

## Your grades

The **Grades** section lists your per-subject averages, period by
period, with your annual average shown at the top of the section.

## Downloading your report cards

The **Report cards** section lists your published report cards, period
by period. Click **Download** next to a period to get your report card
as a PDF. A report card only appears here once the school has published
it.

## Lesson log

The **Lesson log** section lists the lessons given, with date, subject,
content and homework assigned. When a lesson has an attached document, an
**Attachment** link appears next to that row — click it to open it.

## Timetable

The **Timetable** section shows your weekly schedule (day, start time,
end time, subject). To import it into your own calendar (Google Calendar,
Apple Calendar, Outlook…):

1. Click **Export to calendar**.
2. An `.ics` file is downloaded; open it or import it into your usual
   calendar app.

## Boarding

If you board at the school, a **Boarding** section shows your site, room
and bed, along with your boarding attendance.

## Attendance and discipline

The **Attendance** section shows each day with its status (Present,
Absent, Late) and the justification note if there is one. Justifying an
absence is done by your parent or guardian from the parent portal — you
cannot do it yourself from your space.

The **Disciplinary incidents** section lists incidents concerning you
(date, severity, description, sanction), and **Disciplinary councils**
those held for you, with their decision.
