# Online payments

This page explains how to track your child's school fees and settle a
payment directly online, without visiting the school.

## Choosing the year's services

On your child's record, the **Payments** section shows **This year's
fees**:

- the **required** fees (tuition, for instance), always due;
- the **optional services** the school offers (cafeteria, transport…),
  each with its price and a checkbox.

Tick the services your child takes this year, then click **Save my
choices**: the **Total due** and the **Balance** include them straight
away, and you can pay.

You can add a service at any time. A service marked **already paid for** —
a payment was recorded since you ticked it — can no longer be removed from
your space: to stop it, ask the school's cash desk. Until you have paid
anything, you can change your choices.

## Tracking school fees

The same section shows four figures:

- **Total due** — the total fee amount for the year: the required fees and
  the services chosen.
- **Discount** — the amount of any discounts granted.
- **Total paid** — what has already been paid, online or at the school.
- **Balance** — what's still owed.

If an installment plan has been set up, a **Payment plan** table lists
each installment with its label, amount, due date and status (**Paid**,
**Overdue** or **Upcoming**).

The full payment history is shown in a second table, with the date,
amount, method (mobile money, card, bank deposit…), the fee item involved
and the payment's status (**Paid**, **Pending** or **Failed**). This
history includes both your online payments and those collected in person
at the school.

## Paying online by mobile money

As long as a balance remains, a **Pay online** button appears above the
payment figures — if your school has turned online payment on. Without
that button, pay the fees at the school. To make a payment:

1. Click **Pay online**.
2. In the **Pay by mobile money** window, choose the **Network** to use
   (Flooz or Mixx by Yass, formerly T-Money).
3. Enter the **Phone number** that will receive the payment request.
4. Enter the **Amount** to pay — it cannot exceed the remaining balance.
5. Click **Send payment request**.
6. Confirm the request you receive on your phone through your mobile
   money operator.

The payment immediately appears in the history with status **Pending**,
then switches to **Paid** once the transaction is confirmed. If it fails,
it appears as **Failed** and you can try again.

## Downloading a receipt

Once a payment is confirmed, a **Download receipt** link appears next to
it in the history — click it to get the receipt as a PDF.
