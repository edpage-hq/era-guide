# App settings

**App settings**, in the **Administration** group of the left-hand menu,
groups everything that
customises your ERA instance: its visual identity, what gets printed on
receipts, and two settings that genuinely change how the app behaves for
everyone else.

These apply to the whole school, not to your own account. For your personal
preferences (theme, language, security), see
[Getting started](/en/guide/prise-en-main).

## Identity

The **application name** replaces "ERA" everywhere in the interface, in
emails and on generated documents. It's what lets a school show its own
name rather than the software's.

## Colors

A **primary** and a **secondary** color, applied across the interface. The
**Reset** button returns to ERA's defaults if an experiment doesn't work
out — worth trying before hand-correcting a shade.

## Logo

Two separate logos, and the difference matters:

- The **full logo** appears in the expanded menu and on the home page. It's
  also the one printed on the bulletins and payment receipts of any site
  that has no logo of its own (see
  [School structure](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement)).
- The **icon logo** appears in the collapsed menu. If you leave it empty,
  the full logo is used instead — which usually becomes unreadable once
  scaled down. Prepare a square version.

## Home page tagline

The homepage tagline and subtitle are no longer edited here: they joined
the rest of the page's content, in [Homepage](/en/guide/admin/page-accueil).

## Receipt customisation

These fields are printed on **every generated payment receipt**, alongside
the logo (the student's site logo if it has one, otherwise the full logo):
address, phone, email, thank-you message and footer note. These fields are
shared by every site.

This is where the details a family needs on their proof of payment belong.
Already-generated receipts aren't rewritten: the change applies to receipts
produced afterwards.

## Parent self-service linking

This setting decides whether a parent can **search for an already-enrolled
student and link themselves** to that record.

- **On**: the parent does it themselves from their portal (see
  [My children and their documents](/en/guide/parent/enfants-et-documents)).
- **Off**: the option disappears from the parent portal. Linking must then
  be done by the school, from the student's record (see
  [Students and guardians](/en/guide/admin/eleves-tuteurs)).

Check this setting before concluding that a parent "can't find" the
feature: it may simply be turned off for the school.

## Grading

This setting decides **how a subject average combines marks from different
evaluation types** (assignment, exam, quiz…).

- **Off** (the default): each mark counts according to its evaluation
  type's weight, as defined in
  [School structure](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement).
- **On**: each evaluation type counts equally, regardless of how many marks
  it holds. Three quizzes then don't outweigh a single exam.

This is a pedagogical choice, not a display detail: it changes computed
averages, and therefore report cards. Settle it before the year's first
report cards are published, and avoid changing it mid-year.
