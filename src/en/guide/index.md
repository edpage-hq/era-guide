# What's your role?

Every role has its own guide, focused on what it can actually do in ERA.
If you're new to the app, start with [Getting started](/en/guide/prise-en-main)
(signing in, profile, security, notifications) — common to every role.

| Role                                   | What it lets you do                                                               |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Administrator](/en/guide/admin/)      | Configure the school, manage staff, students, fees, timetables and report cards   |
| [Front desk](/en/guide/secretariat/)   | Process applications and site-transfer requests                                   |
| [Cash desk](/en/guide/caissier/)       | Enroll a student, take payments, manage expenses and export accounting data       |
| [Teacher](/en/guide/enseignant/)       | Enter grades, write appreciations, keep the lesson log and homeroom register      |
| [School life](/en/guide/vie-scolaire/) | Track attendance, discipline, cafeteria, transport and extracurricular activities |
| [Boarding](/en/guide/internat/)        | Manage beds and attendance in the boarding house                                  |
| [Infirmary](/en/guide/infirmerie/)     | Log and track students' medical visits                                            |
| [Parent](/en/guide/parent/)            | Follow your children, download their documents, pay online and handle requests    |
| [Student](/en/guide/eleve/)            | View your own space, documents and timetable                                      |

Looking for a school's online signup or the internal edPage panel instead?
That's under [Era Admission](/en/admission/).
