# Students and guardians

This section covers a student's record and everything attached to it:
their class enrollment, their guardians (parents), their possible personal
access to the student portal, and their specialty subjects.

## Student records

**Students & admissions → Students** lists the school's students, with a
search by name and a filter by class (including "Unassigned" for students
with no class in the current year).

To create a student:

1. Click **New student**.
2. Fill in the **First name**, **Last name**, and **Date of birth**
   (optional).
3. Choose a **Class** for the current academic year, or leave it
   unassigned to create the record without an immediate enrollment.
4. Click **Save**.

Editing a student shows the same fields, plus two read-only tables if the
student has history:

- **Enrollment history** — the classes attended in previous academic
  years.
- **Curriculum history** — changes of pedagogical curriculum (French ↔
  Togolese) the student went through, with the date and who made the
  change.

From a student's record, three buttons open dedicated screens: **Specialty
subjects**, **Guardians**, and **Student account** (detailed below).

## Guardians (parents)

From a student's record, click **Guardians** to manage who has parent
portal access for this student.

To add a guardian:

1. Click **Add guardian**.
2. Fill in the **Name**, **Email**, **Relationship** (Father, Mother,
   Legal guardian, or Other), and check **Primary contact** if they're the
   main point of contact.
3. Repeat for each additional guardian (**Remove** button to drop one from
   the form).
4. Click **Save changes**.

Each guardian attached this way gets parent portal access to this student
and to any other student they're linked to elsewhere.

## Student account (student portal)

From a student's record, click **Student account**. Personal access to
the student portal is only possible for a student enrolled in high school
or above — for others, the page shows an ineligibility message and
nothing else.

For an eligible student:

- **Create account** — enter an email address and click **Create
  account**; the student can then sign in with it.
- **Update email** — if an account already exists, change the address and
  click **Update email**.
- **Revoke access** — removes the student's personal access without
  touching their record.

## Specialty subjects

From a student's record, click **Specialty subjects**. This screen only
shows something if the student has an active enrollment and their
department has subjects marked "specialty" (see
[School structure](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement)).

For each specialty subject offered:

1. Check the box to select it for this student.
2. Optionally fill in a personal **coefficient** that replaces the
   department's default — otherwise the department's default coefficient
   applies.
3. Click **Save changes**.
