# School life

The school life team follows students' daily life outside the classroom:
attendance, discipline, cafeteria, transport and extracurricular
activities. Some of these pages are also accessible to teachers or the
registrar's office for viewing only; this guide describes the school life
view, which has access to every action.

This guide covers four activities:

| Page                                                                          | What you do there                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Attendance and absences](/en/guide/vie-scolaire/presences-et-absences)       | Take daily attendance for classes, review absence justifications submitted by families               |
| [Discipline and councils](/en/guide/vie-scolaire/discipline-et-conseils)      | Record a disciplinary incident, convene and decide a disciplinary council                            |
| [Cafeteria and transport](/en/guide/vie-scolaire/cantine-et-transport)        | Manage cafeteria menus and attendance, assign students to transport routes and take their attendance |
| [Extracurricular activities](/en/guide/vie-scolaire/activites-extrascolaires) | Take attendance for extracurricular activity sessions                                                |

For anything about your personal account (login, profile, security,
notifications), common to every role, see
[Getting started](/en/guide/prise-en-main).
