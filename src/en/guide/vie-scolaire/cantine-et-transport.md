# Cafeteria and transport

This page covers running the cafeteria (menus and attendance) and tracking
school transport (assigning students to routes and taking their
attendance). The transport routes themselves are created by the
administrator; as school life staff, you assign students to existing
routes and take their attendance at pickup and drop-off.

## Manage cafeteria menus

1. Open **Cafeteria → Cafeteria menus**, then click **New menu**.
2. Choose the **Site** and **Date** concerned.
3. Describe the content in **Menu**.
4. Click **Save**.

The list of menus can be filtered by site. **Edit** lets you fix the date
or content of an already published menu; **Delete** permanently removes
it after confirmation — this cannot be undone.

## Take cafeteria attendance

**Cafeteria → Cafeteria attendance** shows, for the date selected at the
top of the page (the current day by default), the list of students
currently subscribed to the cafeteria. For each one, set the **Status** —
**Ate** or **Absent** — then click **Save** to record the whole day's
attendance at once. If no student is subscribed, the page says so.

## Assign students to transport routes

1. Open **Transport → Route assignments**, then click **New assignment**.
2. Choose the **Student** (only students not yet assigned to a route are
   offered) then the transport **Route**.
3. Click **Save**.

The list of assignments can be filtered by route. **Unassign** removes a
student from their route after confirmation.

## Take transport attendance

1. Open **Transport → Transport attendance**, which lists routes with
   their site. Click **Take attendance** on the relevant route.
2. Choose the **date** and the **direction** of the trip — **Pickup** or
   **Drop-off** — at the top of the page.
3. For each student assigned to the route, set the **Status**: **Present**
   or **Absent**.
4. Click **Save**.

If no student is assigned to the route, the page says so.
