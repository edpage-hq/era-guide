# Getting started

This page applies to every role: signing in, your profile, security,
notifications and language work the same way whether you're an
administrator, a teacher, a parent or a student. The role-specific guides
(left-hand menu) don't repeat this information.

## Signing in

Open your school's address in a browser and enter your email and password
on the sign-in screen. If your school has enabled it, you can also sign in
with a Google or Apple account.

If two-factor authentication is enabled on your account, you'll be asked
for an extra code after your password (see "Two-factor authentication"
below).

Once signed in, you land on your dashboard — its content depends on your
role.

## Account settings

The **Settings** menu (from your avatar, at the top of the screen) groups
everything related to your personal account, regardless of your role:

- **Profile** — change your name, email address and profile picture. If
  you change your email, a "Your email address is not verified" message
  appears until you click the link sent to that address.
- **Security** — change your password, enable two-factor authentication,
  manage your passkeys (passwordless sign-in) and review your active
  sessions.
- **Appearance** — choose between light, dark or automatic theme.
- **Language** — choose the app's language (French or English). This
  choice immediately changes every label, including this guide's, if you
  open it from within the app.
- **Notifications** — enable push notifications on this device.

### Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Under **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication**, click
**Enable 2FA**. You'll then be asked for a secret code at every sign-in;
it comes from an authenticator app (TOTP) on your phone, after scanning
the code shown on screen. To turn it off, go back to that same page and
click **Disable 2FA**.

### Active sessions

**Settings → Security → Active sessions** lists every device currently
signed in to your account, with the device, IP address and last activity.
The device you're using right now is marked **Current device**. You can:

- **Sign out** a specific device (it will need to sign in again),
- **Sign out other devices** to close every session except the current
  one — useful if you think a device should no longer have access to your
  account.

### Push notifications

**Settings → Notifications** lets you enable push notifications on the
device you're using. Once enabled, you're notified as soon as something
needs your attention (for example a site-transfer request awaiting your
approval). The **Enable**/**Disable** button toggles this; if it says push
notifications aren't supported, your browser or device doesn't allow them.

## The notification bell

At the top of the screen, the bell shows a badge when you have unread
notifications (for example an application to process, or a request to
approve). Click a notification to open it and mark it as read, or use
**Mark all as read** to clear the list at once.

## Switching language

The language switcher (**Settings → Language**, or the language icon in
the header depending on your role) switches the whole interface between
French and English immediately — no need to sign in again.
