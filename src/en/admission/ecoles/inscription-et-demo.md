# Free signup and full-feature demo

Discovering ERA requires no installation and no commitment: your school
can create its own online space in a few minutes, free for a year,
renewable, then switch to a full-feature demo to evaluate the whole
solution before choosing a license.

## Creating your school's account

Signup happens on Era Admission's public site: click **Create my free
space** on its home page, then fill in a very short form:

1. Your school's name.
2. Your name.
3. An email address.
4. A password.

Submitting it creates **nothing yet**: an email goes out to the address
you just typed, with a link to follow. That link, and only that link,
opens your school's space. This step is what keeps a mistyped address
from costing you your own space — your email address is, after all, the
only way back in if you forget your password.

The link stays valid for two days. If nothing arrives, check your spam
folder, then fill the form in again: the latest link replaces the
previous one. An expired or already-used link simply returns you to the
form, which says so.

Once the link is followed, two things are created automatically:

- **A dedicated space for your school**, reachable at its own address
  (a subdomain built from your school's name, e.g.
  `college-sainte-marie.<era-domain>`).
- **Your administrator account**, with the email address and password
  you chose — this is the account that then manages everything else
  (users, structure, admissions...).

A confirmation page reminds you of your space's address and lets you log
in right away. That address is also emailed to you: keep that message.
It's the one your team will use day to day, and it is where — and only
where — a forgotten password is reset.

There is no account other than yours, and no password is sent to you: you
log in with the email address and password you chose on the form.

## What the free tier covers

The free tier isn't a limited version of all of ERA: it's a single
module, built so you can get started right away —

- the **public application form**, which families can already use from
  day one to submit an application;
- the **read-only application queue**, to keep track of what comes in.

The rest of the application (academic life, finances, family relations,
the reporting dashboard...) isn't accessible on this tier. If you want
to see the application as a whole before committing, that's what the
full-feature demo below is for.

## Activating the full-feature demo

Once logged into your space on the free tier, your administrator account
sees an invitation on its dashboard to activate a full-feature demo: it
temporarily unlocks every ERA module, with no commitment, so you can
evaluate the solution under real conditions before choosing a license.

Activation itself — and how the demo winds down (duration, the
"expiring soon" banner, the return to the free tier) — is covered from the
administrator's point of view in
[Full-feature demo and activity log](/en/guide/admin/demo-et-journal);
this guide doesn't duplicate that content.

In short, what matters before you decide:

- The demo runs for a fixed number of days from activation.
- It can only be activated once, from the free tier — you can't restart
  it indefinitely on your own.
- Once active, it can only be ended early by the edPage team (see
  [the internal edPage panel](/en/admission/staff-edpage/)), not from
  your dashboard.

## What happens when the demo ends

You don't have to watch the calendar: a week before the deadline, an
email warns the school's administrator accounts, with the exact date. A
second message goes out on the day the demo ends.

If no license has been purchased by the time the demo period ends, your
space **automatically returns to the free ERA Admissions tier**: the
public application form and the application queue keep working, and the
other modules lock again. It's the same state a demo manually ended by
the edPage team reaches.

**Nothing is deleted.** What you entered during the demo (classes,
students, grades, payments...) is kept, only hidden while the modules
are locked. It comes back as it was the day you take a license: no
re-entry.

The demo can't be started a second time. To get the whole of ERA back,
see [Moving to a license](/en/admission/ecoles/passage-licence) as soon
as you're ready to commit.
