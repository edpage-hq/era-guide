# Teacher

As a teacher, you only see your own classes and subjects: the ones the
administrator has explicitly assigned to you (on the administration side,
**School setup → Teacher assignments**). If a class or subject doesn't appear in
your dropdowns, no assignment links you to it yet — flag it to your
administrator.

If you are designated **homeroom teacher** for a class, you also get a
consolidated view of that class and its end-of-period general
appreciations, described in the third page below.

This guide covers three activities:

| Page                                                                          | What you do there                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Grades and evaluations](/en/guide/enseignant/notes-et-evaluations)           | Create an evaluation, enter grades, record competency observations         |
| [Report cards and lesson log](/en/guide/enseignant/bulletins-et-cahier-texte) | Write report card appreciations, keep the lesson log                       |
| [Homeroom and schedule](/en/guide/enseignant/registre-et-emploi-du-temps)     | View your homeroom class register (homeroom teacher) and your own schedule |

For anything about your personal account (login, profile, security,
notifications), common to every role, see
[Getting started](/en/guide/prise-en-main).
