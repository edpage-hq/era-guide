# Report cards and lesson log

This page covers two distinct tasks: writing the appreciations that will
appear on your students' report cards, and keeping the lesson log that
tracks what was covered in your sessions and the homework assigned.

## Write per-subject appreciations

For each of your class/subject assignments, you can enter one appreciation
per student for a given academic period.

1. From the **Evaluations** list, click **Bulletins** on the row of an
   evaluation for the relevant class and subject.
2. Choose the **academic period** from the selector at the top of the
   page.
3. For each student, enter their **Appreciation** in the text field
   (optional).
4. Click **Save** — every appreciation in the list is saved at once.

## Write the end-of-period general appreciation (homeroom teacher)

If you are the homeroom teacher of a class, you also enter an
end-of-period general appreciation, separate from the per-subject ones.

1. Open **Teaching → My homeroom class**, then click **End-of-period
   appreciations** next to the relevant class.
2. Choose the **academic period** from the selector at the top of the
   page.
3. For each student, fill in the **General appreciation** and **Conduct**
   fields, and check any relevant flags: **Congratulations**,
   **Compliments**, **Encouragements**, **Work warning**, **Conduct
   warning**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Keep the lesson log

The lesson log tracks, session by session, what was covered in class and
the homework assigned, for all your class/subject assignments.

1. Open **Teaching → Lesson log**, then click **New entry**.
2. Choose the **Class** and **Subject**, then fill in the **Date**.
3. Describe the **Content covered** (required) and, if relevant, the
   **Homework** assigned.
4. Optionally attach a **Document** (PDF, JPG or PNG).
5. Click **Save**.

The list of entries can be filtered by keyword (searching the content) or
by class, and shows each entry's date, class, subject, content and
homework. From this list:

- the **Document** button downloads the attachment if an entry has one;
- **Edit** reopens the entry to correct it (you can replace or remove the
  attached document there);
- **Delete** permanently removes it, after confirmation — this cannot be
  undone.
