# Free signup and full-feature demo

Discovering ERA requires no installation and no commitment: your school
can create its own online space in a few minutes, for free and with no
time limit, then switch to a full-feature demo to evaluate the whole
solution before choosing a license.

## Creating your school's account

Signup happens on ERA's public site, through a very short form:

1. Your school's name.
2. Your name.
3. An email address.
4. A password.

Submitting it automatically creates two things:

- **A dedicated space for your school**, reachable at its own address
  (a subdomain built from your school's name, e.g.
  `college-sainte-marie.<era-domain>`).
- **Your administrator account**, with the email address and password
  you just chose — this is the account that then manages everything
  else (users, structure, admissions...).

A confirmation page reminds you of your space's address and lets you log
in right away. Keep that address — it's the one your team will use day
to day.

## What the free tier covers

The free tier isn't a limited version of all of ERA: it's a single
module, built so you can get started right away —

- the **public application form**, which families can already use from
  day one to submit an application;
- the **read-only application queue**, to keep track of what comes in.

The rest of the application (academic life, finances, family relations,
the reporting dashboard...) isn't accessible on this tier. If you want
to see the application as a whole before committing, that's what the
full-feature demo below is for.

## Activating the full-feature demo

Once logged into your space on the free tier, your administrator account
sees an invitation on its dashboard to activate a full-feature demo: it
temporarily unlocks every ERA module, with no commitment, so you can
evaluate the solution under real conditions before choosing a license.

Activation itself — and how the demo winds down (duration, the
"expiring soon" banner, the read-only freeze) — is covered from the
administrator's point of view in
[Full-feature demo and activity log](/en/guide/admin/demo-et-journal);
this guide doesn't duplicate that content.

In short, what matters before you decide:

- The demo runs for a fixed number of days from activation.
- It can only be activated once, from the free tier — you can't restart
  it indefinitely on your own.
- Once active, it can only be ended early by the edPage team (see
  [the internal edPage panel](/en/admission/staff-edpage/)), not from
  your dashboard.

## What happens when the demo ends

If no license has been purchased by the time the demo period ends,
access to your space automatically switches to **read-only**: you can
still view your data, but no longer change it. It's the same state a
demo manually ended by the edPage team would reach.

This read-only state isn't final right away, but it isn't permanent
either: you're given a **grace period** to choose a license or contact
us. Past that period with no action on your part, the school —
including its data — is **permanently deleted**, with no way to recover
it.

To avoid reaching that point, see
[Moving to a license](/en/admission/ecoles/passage-licence) as soon as
you're ready to commit.
