# Leave requests

Leave is one of the few parts of ERA that doesn't depend on your role:
**every staff member** files their own requests, whether they teach, run
the cash desk, the infirmary or the boarding house. The decision follows
the same rule as the other requests: the direction of the person's site,
the general direction or the administration.

## Filing a request

From **My leave requests**:

1. Pick the **type**: paid leave, unpaid leave or sick leave.
2. Set the **start date** and **end date**.
3. Write the **reason**.
4. Click **Submit request**.

The request is created as **Pending**. There's nothing else to do: the
direction of your site, the general direction and the administration are
told, and see it on their [To approve](/en/guide/direction/a-approuver)
page.

When your request is decided, you get a notification: leave granted, or
rejected with the reason.

## Following your requests

The **History** section of the same page lists your past and current
requests with their status:

| Status   | What it means                                            |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pending  | Filed, not reviewed yet                                  |
| Approved | Granted by the administration                            |
| Rejected | Refused — the rejection reason is shown with the request |

You only ever see your own requests, never a colleague's.

::: tip Filing isn't open to everyone
This page assumes you have a staff record at the school. An account without
one (a parent, a student) has no access to it — see
[Staff and user accounts](/en/guide/admin/personnel-utilisateurs) for how
records are created.
:::

## Deciding on requests (direction and administration)

The direction and the administration get an extra view, **Staff → Leave
requests**. A site director sees the requests of their sites' staff; the
general director and the administrator, those of **all** staff. Pending
requests can also be decided from the
[To approve](/en/guide/direction/a-approuver) page, and show in the
dashboard's tasks. Nobody decides their own request.

For each request:

- **Approve** grants it immediately.
- **Reject** opens a confirmation dialog asking for a **rejection reason**.
  That reason is sent to the person concerned with the decision, and stays
  visible in their own history — which is what saves them from having to
  come and ask why.

An already-decided request stays in the list with its outcome: the history
is a record, nothing is erased.
