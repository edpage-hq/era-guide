# Site transfers

A parent can request, from their own account, to transfer their child
from one of the school's sites to another (for example, from one campus
to another). The **Inter-site transfers** page lists these requests and
lets you carry them through. A request must be approved by both the
origin site and the destination site before the transfer takes effect —
you play both roles.

::: info
This page covers only **student** transfers between two sites. Staff
transfers between sites are a separate feature, reserved for
administrators.
:::

## Viewing the queue

The page shows, for each request, the student concerned, the origin
site, the destination site, and the status (**Pending**, **Approved**,
or **Rejected**). Use the status filter to see only the requests you
care about, then click **Review** to open one.

## Reviewing a request

The detail page shows who **requested** the transfer, the **origin
site**, the **destination site**, the desired **department** on the
new site, and, if one was given, the **reason** for the request.

## Approving a transfer

The form presents two separate sections, one for each site involved:

1. In **Origin site approval**, click **Approve origin**. If this
   section already shows someone's name, that site has already given
   its approval and there's nothing more to do here.
2. In **Destination site approval**, choose the receiving **class** at
   the new site (each class's occupancy is shown, for example
   `18/25`). If the class has reached its capacity, check **Force
   assignment even if the class has reached its capacity** to assign
   it anyway, then click **Approve destination**.

As soon as both sites have given their approval — in either order — the
transfer executes automatically: the student is moved to the new site,
department, and class, with no further action on your part.

::: tip
A simplified version of this page, with no menu or navigation, can open
directly from a link received through a notification (for example on
your phone): it lets you approve the origin, the destination, or reject
the request without navigating the rest of the app.
:::

## Rejecting a request

1. From a pending request's detail page, click **Reject**.
2. Enter the **Rejection reason** (required).
3. Confirm by clicking **Reject** again.

A rejected request can no longer be approved: the family must submit a
new one if they want to try again.
