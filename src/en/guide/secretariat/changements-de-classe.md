# Class changes

A student sometimes needs to move to another class after being placed: a
change of orientation in the first weeks (from 1ère C to 1ère D, for
example), or a correction before class lists are final. The **Class
changes** page, in the **Students & admissions** space, is there for
that. It is open to the secretariat and to administrators.

::: info
A class change stays **on the same site** and **within the same academic
year**. To move a student to another site, use a
[site transfer](/en/guide/secretariat/transferts-sites).
:::

## Moving a student to another class

1. In **Student**, type at least two letters of their first or last name,
   then click the student in the list. Only students enrolled in a running
   year (or one already prepared) appear.
2. Check their **current class**, shown with its department, site and
   year. If it is the wrong student, click **Choose another student**.
3. In **New class**, pick the destination. The list only offers classes
   on the same site and in the same year, with their headcount and
   capacity.
4. Add a **Reason** if you wish, for example "moving to the D stream". It
   stays visible in the history.
5. Click **Change class**.

The student's enrollment is moved, not recreated: their payments, payment
plan and bulletins stay attached to their record.

## Warnings before you confirm

Depending on the situation, a box appears under the chosen class:

- **Grades were already entered** in the original class: they are not
  carried over and no longer count for the student. They are not deleted,
  though, and the number of abandoned grades is kept in the history.
- **The class belongs to another department**: check the student's
  options and fee schedule, which may differ.
- **The class is full**: the button stays disabled until you tick **Place
  the student in this class anyway**.

## When a change is refused

- The chosen class is the one the student is already in.
- The **academic year is closed**: no change is possible any more (see
  closing a year in the administrator guide).

## History

Below the form, the **Change history** lists every change: the date, the
student, the original and destination classes with the year, the reason,
and who made it. The number of abandoned grades appears when there were
any.
