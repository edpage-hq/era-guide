# Cashier

The cashier's office manages a student's financial enrollment once
their application has been approved by the registrar's office: fees
due, payments collected, discounts granted, and payment plans. It also
tracks the school's expenses and produces accounting exports. Your pages
are in the **Students & admissions** and **Finances** spaces:

::: info One site
If your employee profile names a site, ERA only shows you that site:
students, classes, family accounts, payments, expenses and dashboard. A record from another site is neither listed
nor opened. With no site on your profile, you see the whole school. The
administrator sets the site on the
[employee record](/en/guide/admin/personnel-utilisateurs#manage-staff-records-and-contracts).
:::

- **[Enrollment and payments](/en/guide/caissier/inscriptions-et-paiements)**
  — enter a new application for a family visiting in person, track fees
  due per student, collect a payment, print a receipt, set up a payment
  plan, and view discounts granted.
- **[Accounting export](/en/guide/caissier/export-comptable)** — export
  payments and expenses as CSV, SYSCOHADA format, or a per-class
  statement.
- **[Expenses](/en/guide/caissier/depenses)** — record a school expense
  and track its status.

To understand how a student reaches the cashier, see the
[registrar's office guide](/en/guide/secretariat/) — approving an
application is what creates the financial enrollment you later find
under **Billing**.
