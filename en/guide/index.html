What's your role? ​

Every role has its own guide, focused on what it can actually do in ERA. If you're new to the app, start with Getting started (signing in, profile, security, notifications) — common to every role.

RoleWhat it lets you do
AdministratorConfigure the school, manage staff, students, fees, timetables and report cards
Front deskProcess applications and site-transfer requests
Cash deskEnroll a student, take payments, manage expenses and export accounting data
TeacherEnter grades, write appreciations, keep the lesson log and homeroom register
School lifeTrack attendance, discipline, cafeteria, transport and extracurricular activities
BoardingManage beds and attendance in the boarding house
InfirmaryLog and track students' medical visits
ParentFollow your children, download their documents, pay online and handle requests
StudentView your own space, documents and timetable

Leave requests are the exception: every staff member files their own, whatever their role.

Looking for a school's online signup or the internal edPage panel instead? That's under Era Admission.
