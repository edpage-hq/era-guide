Infirmary ​

As the person in charge of the infirmary, you record in ERA every student visit to the infirmary: the reason, the care given, and the outcome (returned to class or sent home). The left-hand menu gives you access to one page, Infirmary visits, which lists every visit and lets you create, view, or edit them.

Viewing visits ​

The Infirmary visits page lists recorded visits, with the date and time, the student concerned, the reason, and the outcome (shown as a badge: Returned to class or Sent home, the latter highlighted in red). You can search for a visit by student name using the search field at the top of the page. Click a row to open a summary panel showing the date and time, the reason, and the outcome.

Recording a new visit ​

  1. Click New visit at the top of the Infirmary visits page.
  2. Select the Student concerned.
  3. Fill in the Date and time of the visit.
  4. Describe the Reason for the visit (what brought the student to the infirmary).
  5. The Care given field is optional: describe the care provided, if any.
  6. Choose the Outcome: Returned to class if the student goes back to class, or Sent home if they need to go home.
  7. Click Save.

If you choose the Sent home outcome, the student's guardians automatically receive a notification with the reason and time of the visit — you don't need to do anything else on your end.

Viewing and editing an existing visit ​

  1. From the Infirmary visits page, click Edit on the row for the visit in question (or first open the summary panel by clicking the row, then click Edit from that panel).
  2. Update the student, date and time, reason, care given, or outcome as needed.
  3. Click Save changes.

If you change the outcome to Sent home when it wasn't already, the student's guardians are notified at the moment you save the change, just as for a new visit.

Deleting a visit ​

From the Infirmary visits page, click Delete on the row for the visit in question, then confirm in the dialog that appears. This permanently deletes the record and cannot be undone.
