# Direction

Two profiles are meant for the people who run the school without doing its
day-to-day data entry:

- the **site director** — the head of a campus. They are attached to one or
  more sites and decide what concerns them;
- the **general director** — the head of the school or its founder. They
  see every site and may decide everything.

These profiles are part of the **Pilotage** module. The administrator
assigns them, from
[Staff and user accounts](/en/guide/admin/personnel-utilisateurs).

::: info What the direction does not see
A director has no access to the operational screens: cash desk, grades,
application files, settings. They see the requests waiting for their
decision and, with the upcoming Pilotage lot, their sites' indicators.
:::

## Your space

When you sign in, the **Dashboard** lists what is waiting for your
decision, with the number of pending requests. The **Direction** space of
the menu gathers the three queues:

- **Inter-site transfers** — students moving between two sites;
- **Staff transfers** — an employee changing site;
- **Expenses** — the sites' expense requests.

A site director only sees the requests touching **their** sites. The
general director sees everything.

## Who decides what

| Request          | Site director                                      | General director  |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Student transfer | approves their site's side (leaving or arriving)   | may approve both  |
| Staff transfer   | decides when either site is theirs                 | decides           |
| Expense          | decides up to the school's **threshold**, included | decides, no limit |

The administrator can always decide everything.

A site **without a director** works as before: the secretariat approves
its side of student transfers, and the administration decides staff
transfers and expenses. The same goes when the director's account is
deactivated.

## Student transfers

A transfer must be approved by the site the student leaves **and** the
site they join. Open the request with **Review**:

- your site's block offers **Approve origin** or **Approve destination**;
  for the destination, choose the receiving class;
- the other site's block reads **Waiting for this site's approval** until
  it has answered.

The transfer runs as soon as both sides are approved, in any order. You
can also **Reject** the request, with a reason.

## Staff transfers

One decision is enough: **Approve** applies the change of site, **Reject**
asks for a reason. Both sites' directions are told; the first one to
decide closes the request.

## Expenses

Open an expense to see its amount, description and supporting document,
then **Approve** or **Reject**.

Above the threshold, the page says so — _Amount above the site direction's
approval threshold_ — and the decision goes to the general direction or an
administrator. You are then not notified of those expenses, but you see
them in your sites' list.

The administrator sets this threshold in
[Application settings → Approvals](/en/guide/admin/parametres-application#approvals).

## Classes over capacity

When a class of your site takes a student while it is already full, you
receive a _Class over capacity_ notification: who forced the assignment,
the headcount reached and the **reason** given. There is nothing to
approve: it is information, so these overrides no longer go unnoticed.

## Getting requests on your phone

In **Settings → Notifications**, turn on notifications on your phone. Each
new request then arrives as a notification; tapping it opens a simplified
screen, with no menu, and the buttons to decide.
