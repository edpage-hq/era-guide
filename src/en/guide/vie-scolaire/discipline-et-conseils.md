# Discipline and councils

This page covers recording disciplinary incidents and, when an incident
warrants it, convening and then deciding a disciplinary council.

## Record a disciplinary incident

1. Open **School life → Discipline incidents**, then click **New
   incident**.
2. Choose the **Student** involved and, if relevant, their **Class**
   (leave it blank if there's no specific class to note).
3. Enter the **Date** of the incident and its **Severity**: **Minor**,
   **Major** or **Severe**.
4. Describe the incident in **Description** (required) and, if a sanction
   was already applied at the time, note it in **Sanction**.
5. Click **Save**.

The list of incidents can be filtered by student name, class and
severity. From this list, **Edit** reopens the incident and **Delete**
permanently removes it after confirmation — this cannot be undone.

## Convene and manage a disciplinary council

A disciplinary council can be convened from an existing incident or
started on its own.

1. From the **Discipline incidents** list, click **Convene council** on
   the row for the relevant incident (or open **School life → Disciplinary
   councils** directly, then **New council**, to start from a blank
   form).
2. Choose the **Student**, their **Class** if relevant, and the **Related
   incident** if the council follows a specific incident — the reason then
   pre-fills with the incident's description, which you can edit.
3. Fill in the council's **Reason** and its **Session date**.
4. Build the **Council members** list: for each one, choose the person
   from staff and their role (**President**, **Secretary** or **Member**);
   use **Add member** to include several.
5. Click **Save** — the council is created with the status **Scheduled**.

On a scheduled council's page, you can still:

- **Edit** its details before the session;
- **Cancel council** (with confirmation, this cannot be undone);
- **Delete** the council (with confirmation, this cannot be undone);
- **Record decision**: choose the **Decision** — Warning, Reprimand,
  Temporary exclusion, Permanent exclusion or No sanction — and add
  **Decision details** if needed, then confirm.

Once the decision is recorded, the council moves to the status
**Decided**: the decision and its date are shown, and a **Download
minutes** button generates the corresponding document. A decided or
cancelled council can no longer be edited.

The list of councils can be filtered by student name, class and status
(Scheduled, Decided, Cancelled); the front desk can view and download
minutes but cannot create or decide a council.
