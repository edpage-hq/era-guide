# Era Admission

Era Admission is the channel through which a school discovers and adopts
ERA before, eventually, running its own server. This isn't the day-to-day
of a school that's already set up — for that, see the
[guide by role](/en/guide/).

Two distinct audiences meet here:

- **[Schools](/en/admission/ecoles/inscription-et-demo)** — a school
  discovering ERA signs up online for free, can activate a full-feature
  demo for a limited time, then move to a paid license (à la carte, per
  module) with edPage's support.
- **[The edPage team](/en/admission/staff-edpage/)** — edPage's
  sales/support staff have a separate internal panel to manage signed-up
  schools: grant a license, end a demo, purge an expired account.

Neither of these two journeys has anything to do with a school's own user
accounts (admin, teacher, etc. roles): they happen on ERA's central
domain, before a school account even exists.
