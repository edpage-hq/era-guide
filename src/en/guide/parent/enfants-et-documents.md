# Your children and their documents

This page explains how to find your children in ERA, follow their school
record and download every document about them.

## Seeing your children

The **My children** page (left-hand menu) lists every child linked to
your account, each as a card showing their class, annual average and
payment balance. Click a card to open that child's full record.

If no child shows up yet, your school needs to link your account to your
child's record first — or you may be able to do it yourself if this
feature is enabled (see below).

## Linking a child to your account

If your school has enabled self-service linking, a **Link a child** entry
appears in the left-hand menu. To link a child who is already enrolled to
your account:

1. Open **Link a child**.
2. In the search form, enter the child's **First name**, **Last name**
   and **Date of birth**, then click **Search**.
3. In the results, click **Link** next to the right child.
4. Choose your **Relationship** (Father, Mother, Legal guardian or
   Other).
5. Check **Primary contact** if you should be the first point of contact
   for this child.
6. Click **Link** to confirm.

The child then appears in the **Already linked children** list on this
same page, and on your **My children** page.

::: info If there's no "Link a child" menu
Your school hasn't enabled self-service linking. Nothing is wrong with your
account: in that case the link is made by the front desk or the
administration, from your child's record. Contact the school with the
child's name, date of birth, and your relationship to them.
:::

## Following a child's record

On a child's record (opened from **My children**), you'll find, in order:

- **Grades** — per-subject averages for each period, with the annual
  average shown at the top of the section.
- **Lesson log** — the list of lessons given, with date, subject, content
  and homework assigned.
- **Timetable** — the child's class weekly schedule (day, start time, end
  time, subject).
- **Boarding** — if your child boards at the school, this section shows
  their site, room and bed, along with their boarding attendance.
- **Attendance** — each day with its status (Present, Absent, Late) and,
  where relevant, the justification note.
- **Disciplinary incidents** — date, severity (Minor, Major, Severe),
  description and any sanction.
- **Disciplinary councils** — the disciplinary council sessions held for
  this child and their decision (warning, reprimand, temporary or
  permanent exclusion, or no sanction).

## Downloading your child's documents

From a child's record, you can download:

- **Report cards** — in the "Report cards" section, click **Download**
  next to the period you want. A report card only appears once the school
  has published it.
- **Payment receipts** — in the payment history (payments section), click
  **Download receipt** next to a payment; this link only appears if a
  receipt was generated for that payment.
- **Disciplinary council minutes** — in the "Disciplinary councils"
  section, click **Download minutes** next to a council that has already
  reached a decision.
- **Lesson-log attachments** — when a lesson-log entry has an attached
  document, an **Attachment** link appears next to that row; click it to
  open or download the file.

## Exporting the timetable

To import your child's timetable into your own calendar (Google Calendar,
Apple Calendar, Outlook…):

1. On the child's record, go to the **Timetable** section.
2. Click **Export to calendar**.
3. An `.ics` file is downloaded; open it or import it into your usual
   calendar app to add your child's classes to your agenda.
