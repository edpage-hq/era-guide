# To approve

The **To approve** page gathers everything waiting on **your** decision:
expenses, student site transfers, staff transfers. It is made for a phone:
each request fits on one card, with its buttons. It opens first in the
**Direction** space of the menu, or from **Home** for an administrator.

## What you find there

Only what you may decide yourself:

- a **site director** sees their sites' expenses up to the
  [threshold](/en/guide/direction/#expenses), the staff transfers touching
  one of their sites, and their sites' side of student transfers;
- the **general director** and the **administrator** see everything;
- the **secretariat** sees the student-transfer side of the sites that
  have no director.

A transfer whose side you have already approved is no longer listed: it is
waiting on the other site. An expense above the threshold does not appear
for a site director, since the general direction decides it.

## Deciding

- **Approve** settles the request in one gesture.
- **Reject** asks for a reason, passed on to the requester.
- **Details** opens the request's full page.

A decided request leaves the list straight away.

### Welcoming a student

For a transfer, the departure site **approves the departure**, the arrival
site **approves the arrival**. Approving the arrival asks for the class:
only the classes of the right department, on the arrival site and in the
current year, are offered, with their headcount (for instance
_6e A (30/30)_).

To place the student in a full class, tick **Force the assignment** and give
the reason. The override is recorded and shows on the
[Pilotage](/en/guide/direction/pilotage#classes-over-capacity) page.

The transfer is carried out once both sites have approved, in either order.

## The money at a glance

At the top of the page, the direction and the administrator see, over
their sites and for the year chosen in the header:

- what was **Collected** and **Disbursed** this month;
- the year's **Collection** rate, with the amount still to collect;
- the total of the **Expenses to decide** waiting on you.

The **Open the pilotage** link leads to the details.

## From a notification

Turn notifications on in **Settings → Notifications**. Each new request
then reaches your phone; a tap opens the **To approve** page with that
request outlined.

If someone decided it in the meantime, a message says so, with an **Open
the request** link to see what was decided.
