# Re-enrollments

Once the [year-end decisions](/en/guide/admin/decisions-fin-annee) are
taken, re-enroll students into the next year, class by class: those who
move up into a class of the next level, those who repeat into a class of
the same level. ERA creates a **new enrollment** for each of them in the
new year; their enrollment in the past year, with its grades, bulletins
and payments, stays untouched.

## Before you start

1. **Create and prepare the next year**: its classes and fee structures
   (see
   [Preparing the next year](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement#preparing-the-next-year)).
   Add the classes that didn't exist yet, e.g. the final-year classes the
   current top classes move up into.
2. **Check the levels**: every class of the past year and of the new year
   needs its level (see [Levels](/en/guide/admin/structure-etablissement#levels)).
3. **Take the year-end decisions**: a student without a decision is not
   re-enrolled.

## Re-enrolling a class

1. Open **Students & admissions → Re-enrollments**.
2. Choose **From** (the past year), **To** (the new year) and the
   **Class**. The class list shows how many students remain to place.
3. Students are grouped by decision:
   - **Moving up to …**: choose their class in the new year, among classes
     of the next level on the same site;
   - **Repeating …**: choose their class among classes of the same level.
4. Click **Re-enroll n students into …**.

When only one class fits, it is already selected. When several do, ERA
selects the one sharing the source class's **series** if there is one;
otherwise, choose.

Not re-enrolled:

- students who **leave the school**, and students who pass the school's
  last level;
- students **without a decision**: a **Decide now** link leads to their
  class's decisions;
- students **already enrolled** in the new year, e.g. placed by hand: they
  are never moved. Running the re-enrollment again is therefore safe.

## What follows the student

- Their **specialty subjects** are carried into the new enrollment when the
  new class still offers them as a choice. Their personal coefficient is
  kept within the same department; in another department, that
  department's coefficient applies.
- Their **optional fees** (cafeteria, transport…) are not carried over: the
  family chooses them again every year. The cashier sees last year's and
  can pick them again in one click, see
  [Enrollments and payments](/en/guide/caissier/inscriptions-et-paiements#setting-the-fees-due).

## Unpaid balances

Each student who still owes money for the past year is marked **Unpaid**
with the remaining amount. Re-enrollment is **not blocked**: the balance is
settled at the cash desk.

## Capacity

If a chosen class would go over its capacity, tick **Go over the capacity
of …** (the checkbox names the class) to re-enroll anyway.

## Afterwards

- A student placed in the wrong class is moved with a
  [class change](/en/guide/secretariat/changements-de-classe) in the new
  year; a student changing site goes through a
  [site transfer](/en/guide/secretariat/transferts-sites).
- A re-enrolled student's decision can no longer be changed or cancelled:
  the decisions page shows "Re-enrolled in …".
- When the new school year starts, switch the current year with **Set as
  current** under **School setup → Academic years**.

::: tip New students
New students come in through [applications](/en/guide/secretariat/candidatures):
an application approved in June can already go into a class of the next
year.
:::
