# Transcripts and certificates

In a higher education institution, the secretariat prints the documents
juries have published: **transcripts**, session by session, and
**certificates of completion**. Open **Higher education → Transcripts and
certificates**. The administrator can use it too.

Only published results appear here: a deliberation in progress, or
reopened, prints nothing until the jury closes it.

## For one student, at the desk

Type their **name** or **registration number**, then **Search**. For each
student found, the page lists:

- their certificate of completion, if they completed their programme;
- their transcripts, latest first: promotion, year and session (first
  session or retake).

**Transcript** or **Certificate** downloads the document as a PDF.

## For a whole promotion

Under **By promotion**, choose the **year**. Each promotion with a
published session offers:

- **Transcripts — First session** and **Transcripts — Retake**: every
  student's transcript, a page each;
- **Certificates**: the certificates already issued to its graduates.

The secretariat prints certificates but does not issue them: the
administrator or the programme's head does, from the promotion's
**Graduates** page.
